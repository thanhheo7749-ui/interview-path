# 🎤 InterviewPath + SpeakCV — Presentation Script

**Total Time:** ~7–8 minutes  
**Navigation:** Click the **right half** of the screen → next slide. Click **left half** → previous slide. You can also use **← →** arrow keys.  
**Tone:** Inspiring, confident, professional. Speak like an expert sharing a breakthrough.

---

## SLIDE 1 — Title & Opening ⏱️ ~45 seconds

> **What's on screen:** Title "InterviewPath + SpeakCV" / "The Connected Hiring Journey."

### 🗣️ Say This:

> "Hello everyone, and thank you for being here.
>
> Today, I want to introduce you to **InterviewPath**. This is our larger AI platform designed to transform the entire hiring journey — from the moment we understand a candidate, to interview support, all the way to the final hiring decision.
>
> But for today's presentation, I am going to focus on one of the clearest and most exciting parts inside that platform: **SpeakCV**.
>
> SpeakCV is the feature that proves the value of our platform in a very direct way. So, I will start with the big picture of InterviewPath, and then we will zoom in on SpeakCV to see it in action."

**💡 Tip:** Smile and make eye contact. Speak slowly and clearly. Emphasize the names "InterviewPath" and "SpeakCV".

---

## SLIDE 2 — The Problem & Big Idea ⏱️ ~45 seconds

> **What's on screen:** "The Problem: Fragmented Hiring" vs "The Big Idea: Candidate Passport."

### 🗣️ Say This:

> "Let's start with the problem we are solving.
>
> On the company side, hiring teams deal with too many CVs, too much manual screening, and not enough structured feedback.
>
> On the candidate side, people don't know if they truly fit the role. They don't know how to prepare, and they rarely get useful feedback after an interview. Both sides are frustrated by a process that is fragmented and manual.
>
> That is where InterviewPath comes in. Our big idea is simple: **Hiring should not depend on only one CV or one interview.**
>
> Instead, we build a richer, continuous understanding of the candidate using a **Candidate Passport**. Think of it as a living profile that grows over time — capturing interview signals, strengths, weak areas, and real progress."

**💡 Tip:** Use hand gestures to weigh the two sides — "Company side" (left hand) and "Candidate side" (right hand). Pause slightly after "Candidate Passport".

---

## SLIDE 3 — The Bigger Platform View ⏱️ ~40 seconds

> **What's on screen:** "The Platform Vision: A Connected Journey."

### 🗣️ Say This:

> "At a high level, InterviewPath is not just one isolated AI chat. It is a system that organizes the candidate flow, connects candidate skills with job requirements, and creates better evaluation experiences.
>
> The story of the platform is this: we start with **candidate context**, we guide the interview process **intelligently**, and we keep useful **signals** to support better hiring decisions later.
>
> That is the larger frame. And inside that frame, SpeakCV is the absolute best part to demonstrate live."

**💡 Tip:** Count out the three steps on your fingers: 1 (context), 2 (guide intelligently), 3 (keep signals).

---

## SLIDE 4 — Transition to SpeakCV ⏱️ ~30 seconds

> **What's on screen:** "Zooming in on SpeakCV."

### 🗣️ Say This:

> "So, that is the bigger picture.
>
> Now, I want to zoom in on **SpeakCV**. If a platform is going to be meaningful, it needs a feature that clearly shows real value.
>
> SpeakCV is our clearest proof point. It shows how candidate context, question flow, and feedback can come together in a way that feels practical, powerful, and easy to understand."

**💡 Tip:** Lower your pitch slightly to draw the audience in. This is the transition to the "meat" of the presentation.

---

## SLIDE 5 — SpeakCV: The Gap in the Market ⏱️ ~45 seconds

> **What's on screen:** "Generic Interview Bots" vs "SpeakCV: Focused & Relevant."

### 🗣️ Say This:

> "So, what is SpeakCV? It is an AI interview practice and coaching experience.
>
> But our goal is not just to build another chatbot. A lot of interview tools today can ask and answer questions, but they feel too generic. They ask questions randomly, and the conversation feels disconnected. The next question rarely relates to the candidate's actual background or the job description.
>
> Because of that, users feel like they are just chatting with a robot, not practicing a real interview. And the feedback they get is too broad to be useful.
>
> That is exactly the gap SpeakCV is closing. We are making the interview feel **focused, relevant, and helpful**."

**💡 Tip:** Shake your head slightly when mentioning "generic chatbots". You are highlighting the pain point that makes your product special.

---

## SLIDE 6 — SpeakCV Flow: Context Setup ⏱️ ~40 seconds

> **What's on screen:** "Step 1: Context Matters (CV + JD + Interview Mode)."

### 🗣️ Say This:

> "Let me walk you through how SpeakCV actually works.
>
> First, we begin with **context**. Before the interview even starts, we feed the system the candidate's CV, the job description, and the chosen interview mode.
>
> This step matters because a good interview should **never start from zero**.
>
> If the system understands what role you are targeting and what skills matter, the whole session becomes focused from minute one. Instead of random questions, SpeakCV starts with personalized context. It feels real."

**💡 Tip:** Emphasize the phrase "never start from zero." It's a strong, memorable soundbite.

---

## SLIDE 7 — SpeakCV Flow: Intentional Questions ⏱️ ~50 seconds

> **What's on screen:** "Step 2: The Live Chat & Intentional Follow-ups."

### 🗣️ Say This:

> "After the setup, the live chat begins. The user gives an answer, and the AI responds naturally.
>
> But here is the most important part: when the AI replies, it doesn't just pull a random question from a list. It asks a question **connected to what just happened**.
>
> For example: If the candidate gives a strong answer, the system goes deeper. If the answer is too general, it asks for detail. If there is a gap compared to the job description, it probes that gap.
>
> Our goal is simple: SpeakCV tries to ask the **right** next question, not just the next *possible* question. It uses structured signals from the conversation to keep the interview focused and consistent."

**💡 Tip:** This is the technical core. Speak with conviction. Stress the difference between the "right" question and the "possible" question.

---

## SLIDE 8 — SpeakCV Flow: Coaching & Report ⏱️ ~40 seconds

> **What's on screen:** "Step 3: Turning Interviews into Learning."

### 🗣️ Say This:

> "Finally, what happens after the conversation ends?
>
> A useful tool shouldn't just say 'goodbye'. Users want to leave with something they can actually improve.
>
> That is why SpeakCV turns the interview into **useful coaching**. After the session, the user gets a clear summary of their performance. We highlight their strong areas, their weak areas, and the exact skills they need to practice next.
>
> This turns a one-time chat into a true **learning experience**."

**💡 Tip:** Sound supportive and empathetic here. You are talking about helping candidates grow.

---

## SLIDE 9 — Reconnecting to InterviewPath ⏱️ ~30 seconds

> **What's on screen:** "SpeakCV + InterviewPath = The Full Journey."

### 🗣️ Say This:

> "Now that we've seen SpeakCV closely, let's connect it back to the bigger platform.
>
> SpeakCV is incredibly useful by itself as a coach. But inside **InterviewPath**, it becomes even more valuable.
>
> All those signals, the feedback, the progress, and the structured interview memory — they flow back into the larger journey. SpeakCV is not separate. It is one of the most visible ways the InterviewPath platform becomes real and practical."

**💡 Tip:** Bring your hands together to visually show the connection between SpeakCV and InterviewPath.

---

## SLIDE 10 — Closing & Q&A ⏱️ ~30 seconds

> **What's on screen:** Big Title: "InterviewPath". Subtitle: "Strengthening the Whole Hiring Journey."

### 🗣️ Say This:

> "To close, the main message is this:
>
> InterviewPath gives the bigger vision of a connected hiring journey. SpeakCV shows that vision in action through context-aware practice, relevant follow-up questions, and useful coaching.
>
> By making the interview experience more focused, natural, and useful, we are not just improving one chat. We are strengthening the **entire hiring journey**.
>
> Thank you for your time. I am now happy to answer any questions!"

**💡 Tip:** End with a strong, confident tone. Look directly at the judges/audience on the final line. Pause before saying "Thank you."

## 🎯 Final Presenter Tips

1. **Pacing:** Keep the platform explanation (Slides 2-4) moving quickly. Spend your deepest energy and focus on the SpeakCV flow (Slides 6-8).
2. **Don't Memorize:** Use the Cheat Sheet to memorize the "anchor" sentences. Speak the rest naturally.
3. **If You Blank Out:** Just return to the core message: *"InterviewPath is the big platform, and SpeakCV is the live proof inside it."*
4. **Silence is Okay:** Pause for 1-2 seconds between slides. It lets the audience digest your point and makes you look incredibly confident.
