# InterviewPath + SpeakCV Combined Presentation Design

## 1. Goal
Create a revised English presentation script that combines two existing directions:
- a broad InterviewPath platform presentation
- a SpeakCV-focused interview demo script

The revised version should present InterviewPath as the larger end-to-end platform, while making SpeakCV the main proof point and most memorable demo section.

---

## 2. Audience
The audience is mixed:
- non-technical listeners
- semi-technical listeners
- technical listeners
- judges or reviewers who want both product vision and a concrete working demo

Because of this, the script should stay simple, clear, and easy to speak, while still sounding structured and credible.

---

## 3. Core communication strategy
The main message should be:

> InterviewPath is the larger platform, and SpeakCV is the clearest live proof point inside that platform.

This means the script should not treat SpeakCV as a separate product with no relationship to InterviewPath.
It should also not spend so much time on the big platform that SpeakCV loses impact.

The combined script should make the audience understand:
1. why the larger platform exists
2. where SpeakCV fits inside it
3. why SpeakCV is the best part to demonstrate live
4. how SpeakCV strengthens the platform story

---

## 4. Recommended presentation structure

### 4.1 Opening: big vision first
The script should open with InterviewPath as the larger platform.
Very early in the talk, it should set audience expectations with a line such as:

> InterviewPath is the full platform, and today I will focus on one of its most visible parts: SpeakCV.

This keeps the platform identity clear from the beginning.

### 4.2 Problem and insight
The script should keep the HR-side and candidate-side pain points, plus the Candidate Passport / living profile idea.
However, this section should be slightly shorter than the original broad script so it does not delay the main demo.

### 4.3 Platform overview
The script should keep the broad InterviewPath structure:
- the two-engine solution
- the end-to-end journey
- the architecture or system view

But these sections should be explained at a high level only.
The purpose is orientation, not full technical teaching.

### 4.4 Transition to SpeakCV
The script must include a clear transition section that zooms in from platform to proof point.
Example direction:

> That is the big picture of InterviewPath. Now I want to zoom in on one part that makes this value easiest to see in action: SpeakCV.

This is a critical structural element because it turns the broad overview into a focused demonstration.

### 4.5 SpeakCV as the main proof point
The SpeakCV section should become the longest, clearest, and most concrete part of the presentation.
It should include:
- what SpeakCV is
- why many mock interview tools still feel generic
- setup with CV, JD, or interview context
- the live chat flow
- why the next question feels relevant
- coaching or report value after the interview

This is the part where the audience should most strongly feel product value.

### 4.6 Reconnect SpeakCV to InterviewPath
After the SpeakCV demo, the script should reconnect the feature to the larger platform story.
Example direction:

> SpeakCV is not only useful by itself. Inside InterviewPath, it also creates candidate signals, feedback, and progress memory that strengthen the larger hiring workflow.

This ensures the demo supports the platform story, instead of drifting away from it.

### 4.7 Final closing
The closing should return to the full product message:
- InterviewPath is the larger system
- SpeakCV is the clearest proof point shown today
- the candidate passport / talent memory idea remains the long-term differentiator

---

## 5. Tone and language rules
The revised script should:
- use common English words
- keep sentences relatively short
- sound natural and confident
- stay friendly rather than formal
- avoid deep technical detail unless necessary
- remain easy to say aloud in a live presentation

It should sound like a smart student or builder explaining a real product, not like a corporate sales pitch.

---

## 6. Slide handling strategy
The revised script should preserve the broad slide sequence of the InterviewPath presentation, but rebalance the speaking time.

Recommended weighting:
- early platform slides: shorter and cleaner
- transition slide or transition moment: very clear
- SpeakCV section: longest and most detailed
- final closing: reconnect to full platform value

This means the platform slides set context, and the SpeakCV slides deliver the strongest proof.

---

## 7. Scope boundaries
### In scope
- one combined English presentation script
- InterviewPath as platform context
- SpeakCV as central live demo proof point
- mixed-audience wording
- structure suitable for roughly 6-10 minutes, with room to extend slightly if needed

### Out of scope
- rewriting the entire product positioning from zero
- creating a separate second deck
- splitting into multiple script files
- deep technical architecture narration for every system component

---

## 8. Success criteria
The revised script is successful if:
- the audience clearly understands InterviewPath is the larger platform
- the audience clearly remembers SpeakCV as the standout demo section
- the transition from broad platform to focused demo feels smooth
- the ending reconnects the SpeakCV demo back to the larger platform story
- the presenter can speak it naturally in simple English
