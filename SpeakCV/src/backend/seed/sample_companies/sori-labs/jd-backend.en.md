# Backend Developer, Product Engineering (Sori Labs, Sample)

This is a fictional job description for demonstration only.

## About the role

The Backend Developer owns services behind our voice-first learning app: the API, the speech processing pipeline and the data that learners generate. The team is small, so you will decide a lot yourself, write down why, and see your work in front of real learners within days.

## Must-have requirements

- Python development experience of at least 3 years
- FastAPI or a similar async web framework
- PostgreSQL schema design and query optimisation
- Habit of writing short design notes and decision records
- Comfort giving and receiving direct feedback in code review
- Experience running what you ship, including on-call and incident follow-up

## Nice-to-have

- Experience with speech, audio or real-time streaming
- Korean or Vietnamese language ability for working across offices
- Experience with feature flags and automated deployment pipelines

## What a typical week looks like

You write a short update in the team channel each morning, ship small pull requests, and review your teammates' work within the day.
You pick up a problem you noticed, write a one-page proposal and ask for objections.
When you disagree with a decision, you say so in the document and then commit to the outcome.

## What we look for in interviews

We ask about a time you owned a failure, a decision you wrote down, and a time you disagreed with someone senior.
We value candidates who are direct, curious and comfortable without a lot of structure.
