# Sori Labs (Sample): How We Work

This is fictional sample material. It does not describe any real company.

We are a small team building a voice-first learning product. We have few levels, and everyone is expected to decide things in their own area. These values are meant to be seen in daily behaviour.

## Ownership

Whoever finds a problem owns it until it is closed or handed over in writing.
We do not wait for permission to fix something that is clearly broken in our own area.
When we ship something, we stay around to watch it work in production.

## Write it down

A decision does not exist until it is written in the decision log.
We write short design notes before building anything that takes more than two days.
We prefer a clear document to a long meeting, and we share notes with people who were not there.

## Disagree openly

We state objections in the pull request or the proposal, not in the corridor.
Disagreement is a contribution. We thank the person who finds the hole in our plan.
After a decision is made, we commit to it fully, even if our option was not chosen.

## Stay close to the learner

We read learner feedback every week and bring one real quote to planning.
We would rather ship a small improvement this week than a large one next quarter.

## Pull requests and review speed

Every pull request gets a first review within 4 working hours during core hours.
Small pull requests are preferred, and reviewers say what they would do instead of only saying no.
Anyone on the team can approve a change, including the newest engineer.
