# Daehan Precision Vina (Sample): Our Values

This is fictional sample material. It does not describe any real company.

We make precision parts that go into products our customers trust with their own reputation. Our values are not slogans. Each one is a behaviour you can see in a normal week.

## Follow the process

We follow the written procedure even when a shortcut looks safe.
If a procedure is wrong, we do not bend it quietly. We raise a change request and wait for approval.
New engineers are expected to read the relevant procedure before they touch a system.

## Document every decision

Every decision leaves a record in the approval system, with a name and a date.
We write meeting minutes the same day and send them to everyone who attended.
If it is not written down, we treat it as if it did not happen.

## Escalate through the reporting line

We report problems to our direct manager within the same shift.
We do not go around a manager to a senior leader, and we do not hide bad news to protect a schedule.
A manager who receives a report acknowledges it in writing and decides who handles it.

## Quality before speed

We stop the line when a measurement is out of tolerance.
We would rather deliver one day late with a verified result than on time with an open doubt.
A reviewer who finds a defect is thanked, never blamed.

## Respect for seniors and teammates

We address colleagues with the agreed titles and keep meetings on time.
Senior engineers explain the reasons behind a rule when a junior asks.
In code review, we raise at most one review round per change unless the risk is high.
