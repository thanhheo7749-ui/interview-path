# Backend Developer, Manufacturing IT (Daehan Precision Vina, Sample)

This is a fictional job description for demonstration only.

## About the role

The Backend Developer builds and maintains the services behind our production management system. The system tracks work orders, inspection results and traceability records for every batch that leaves the plant. You will report to the Backend team lead and work closely with quality engineers.

## Must-have requirements

- Java development experience of at least 3 years
- Spring Boot experience building REST services
- Oracle database and SQL tuning experience
- Habit of writing design documents before coding
- Ability to follow change-control procedures and approval workflows
- Clear written communication in English, with Korean or Vietnamese

## Nice-to-have

- Familiarity with ISO 9001 quality management
- Experience with manufacturing execution systems
- Korean language ability for working with headquarters

## What a typical week looks like

You attend the 09:00 stand-up, work on tickets that are approved in Jira, and submit your pull requests for review by the team lead and one other engineer.
You write a short daily report for your team lead and a weekly summary for the department head.
When something breaks, you report it to your manager first and write the incident report within the deadline.

## What we look for in interviews

We ask how you document your work, how you handle a procedure you disagree with, and when you escalate a problem to your manager.
We value candidates who are careful, traceable and calm under process.
