# Pitch Script: InterviewPath

**Estimated Duration:** 5 - 7 minutes
**Style:** Confident, empathetic, professional.

## 🟢 SLIDE 1: TITLE (Opening)

*(You walk to the center of the stage, exuding confidence. Slide 1 is on the screen).*

**🗣️ Speaker Notes:**
"Hello judges, ladies, and gentlemen.
How many of you in this room have ever felt your heart pounding and your palms sweating before a crucial interview with a foreign company?
And on the flip side, how many HR professionals here have felt exhausted from reading thousands of CVs just to find a few suitable candidates?
Today, our team brings a solution that completely solves both of these pain points. Introducing:  **InterviewPath - An automated funnel for HR and a practical training ground for Candidates.** "

## 🟢 SLIDE 2: THE PROBLEM

*(Click the right half of the screen to transition to Slide 2)*

**🗣️ Speaker Notes:**
"In the current hiring process, we are facing a massive bottleneck on both sides.
**For HR:** It's overload. Thousands of CVs pour in every day. HR has to spend weeks manually screening them, leading to a slow process and the risk of losing top talent.
**For Candidates:** It's anxiety. This is especially true for young Vietnamese candidates applying to multinational corporations like those in Korea or the US. Many fail not because they lack technical skills, but because of unstructured communication, language barriers, and being completely 'in the dark' about corporate culture."

## 🟢 SLIDE 3: THE KEY INSIGHT

*(Click the right half of the screen to transition to Slide 3 - Pause for a beat, speak slowly and emphasize)*

**🗣️ Speaker Notes:**
"From that reality, we realized a core truth - an Insight that many HR tools today are missing:
The real hiring gap is **not only skill mismatch.**
**It is interview readiness mismatch.**
Rejecting a brilliant engineer just because they don't know how to present themselves humbly according to Korean corporate standards, or because they struggle with professional English... is an enormous waste of talent for the company itself."

## 🟢 SLIDE 4: THE SOLUTION CONCEPTS

*(Click the right half of the screen to transition to Slide 4)*

**🗣️ Speaker Notes:**
"To radically solve this problem, InterviewPath leverages the power of AI, acting as your  **Digital HR Team** . We use two core technologies:
First, **n8n - Acting as a traffic controller.** Instead of humans manually downloading CVs, reading them, and sending emails, n8n automates this entire workflow in seconds.
Second, **RAG - Acting as an 'Open-Book Brain'.** Unlike generic AI models that give generic answers, we feed your company's specific 'culture handbook' and technical requirements directly into RAG. As a result, our AI only asks and coaches exactly what your company actually needs."

## 🟢 SLIDE 5: SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE (The Big Picture)

*(Click the right half of the screen to transition to Slide 5 - Gesture smoothly from left to right, avoid over-explaining technical jargon)*

**🗣️ Speaker Notes:**
"And here is how the system operates.
Looking from the left, InterviewPath takes 3 core inputs: **Candidate CV, Job Description (JD), and Company Culture.**
All this data is pushed into the coordination center: the n8n automation system. Here, n8n combines with the database and the power of AI to analyze, make decisions, and branch into two distinct outputs:
One is a **Match Report Dashboard** for HR.
Two is a **Practical Practice Platform** exclusively for Candidates."

## 🟢 SLIDE 6: HR WORKFLOW

*(Click the right half of the screen to transition to Slide 6)*

**🗣️ Speaker Notes:**
"For the HR workflow, speed is the number one priority.
As soon as a candidate submits a CV, the AI immediately extracts their skills and compares them against the JD.
If the match is low, the system automatically sends a polite rejection email.
But if the match is high, the candidate instantly receives an email containing a 'golden ticket' – a secure link inviting them to join the internal interview practice platform. This instant experience helps companies retain top talent immediately ahead of competitors."

## 🟢 SLIDE 7: CANDIDATE EXPERIENCE

*(Click the right half of the screen to transition to Slide 7 - Speak with an inspiring tone)*

**🗣️ Speaker Notes:**
"But what truly differentiates InterviewPath is the Candidate Experience. We call this the  **'Polish & Shadow' flow** .
As soon as the candidate clicks the link, the AI reads the past projects directly from their CV to ask scenario-based questions.
When the candidate gives an unstructured English answer, the AI doesn't reject them. It acts as a Mentor, improving grammar, and rewriting the answer to be highly professional and perfectly aligned with the company culture. Then, it asks the candidate to shadow the new pronunciation to build muscle memory and confidence."

## 🟢 SLIDE 8: DEMO SCENARIO

*(Click the right half of the screen to transition to Slide 8 - Change your tone slightly to sound like a storyteller)*

**🗣️ Speaker Notes:**
"Let's look at a real-life example. Here is a Backend Intern candidate applying to a Korean IT company.
The AI presents a scenario: *"Tell me about a time you handled deadline pressure."*
The candidate answers honestly but unprofessionally: *"I tried my best and asked my teammate when I had problems."*
Immediately, the AI provides feedback: *"Your answer is understandable, but too general. Use the STAR method."* And boom! The AI provides a 'Polished' answer: *"In my university project, our team had three days to fix an API bug... I checked the backend logic, worked with the frontend... Finally, we fixed the issue before the deadline."*
That is the transformation that InterviewPath delivers."

## 🟢 SLIDE 9: EXPECTED IMPACT

*(Click the right half of the screen to transition to Slide 9)*

**🗣️ Speaker Notes:**
"And with this solution, the expected impact is a Win-Win.
**For HR Teams:** We reduce manual CV screening time, speed up the shortlisting process, and most importantly, identify candidates who are a true cultural 'Fit' earlier.
**For Candidates:** They are better prepared, more confident, and develop a highly favorable, professional view of your employer brand."
