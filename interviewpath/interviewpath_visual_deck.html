01
The Problem

Recruitment is Fragmented

For HR Teams

CVs are reviewed manually. Interview scheduling requires endless back-and-forth emails. Feedback from multiple rounds is scattered, and valuable candidate history is lost.

For Candidates

They don't know if they truly fit the job. They are unprepared for specific corporate cultures, receive zero feedback after failing, and cannot track their own progress.

02
Key Insight

The Missing Memory Layer

"Hiring should not be based on a single CV or interview.
It requires an AI Candidate Passport."

Breakthrough: InterviewPath creates a Candidate Digital Twin — a living AI profile that remembers each candidate’s skills, interview answers, culture-fit signals, feedback history, and readiness progress.

03
The Solution

InterviewPath Platform

A unified AI Recruitment Journey Platform driven by two core engines.

Workflow Automator (n8n)

Controls the entire lifecycle automatically: from receiving CVs, sending practice links, scheduling calendar invites, to moving profiles from applicant to employee record.

Company Hiring Brain (RAG)

Stores company culture, rubrics, and technical requirements. Generates personalized interview coaching, evaluates culture fit, and provides detailed candidate readiness reports.
